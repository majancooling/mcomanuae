Our Story

Majan Cooling Co. was founded in 2005 as a small air conditioning service company, initially serving limited areas in Darsait and Ruwi, Oman. What started as a humble operation with a passion for excellence has grown into a trusted name across the entire Sultanate of Oman and now extends to the United Arab Emirates.

Over the years, our dedication to quality and customer satisfaction has driven our remarkable growth. Today, we proudly serve 300+ satisfied customers with our comprehensive AC solutions. Our expansion from a neighborhood service provider to serving almost all of Oman and now the UAE reflects our commitment to delivering exceptional cooling solutions across the Gulf region.

What makes us unique is our unwavering commitment to premium quality. We never cut corners. Instead, we exclusively partner with the best brands and use only top-tier components for every installation and repair. Our team of highly skilled technicians ensures that every job is completed with utmost care and responsibility.

Most importantly, we deliver all these premium services at affordable prices, making quality AC solutions accessible to everyone. This is our promise to every customer—exceptional quality without breaking the bank.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide the best air conditioning services at affordable prices, ensuring that premium cooling solutions are accessible to every customer in Oman and the UAE.

We believe in building a workplace where our employees thrive—with fair compensation, collaborative workspaces that encourage teamwork, and abundant growth opportunities. Our team is our strength, and we invest in their development.

We are committed to delivering high-end quality products for durability and excellent service that ensures long-lasting excellence in air conditioning systems. Every job we undertake reflects our dedication to customer satisfaction and responsibility.

Our Vision

Our vision is to bring comfort and coolness to every home and business in Oman and the UAE at affordable rates, making quality of life enhancements accessible to all.

We aspire to be the trusted partner for AC solutions across the Gulf region—known for delivering budget-conscious, high-quality work that doesn't compromise on excellence or service.

We envision a future where every customer in Oman and the UAE can enjoy reliable, professional air conditioning services with the confidence that they're receiving the best quality at the most reasonable price in the market.
